SAFARI · YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

Tarangire & Ngorongoro in two days

A short northern escape: Tarangire’s elephant country, a night around Karatu, then the crater floor.

2 days · 1 nightPrivate 4×4 · modest lodgeArusha → Arusha

THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Picture yourself here.

TarangireNgorongoroKaratu
Day 1Tarangire, then Karatu

Leave Arusha early for Tarangire game drives and a picnic lunch. Continue to Karatu for dinner and one lodging night.

Day 2Descend into the Ngorongoro Crater

Drive to the conservation area and descend for the permitted crater game drive. Have a picnic lunch and return to Arusha in the evening.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE PLANNING

A clear view of the details.

These are the planned inclusions for this itinerary. David confirms the exact services and total in your personal quote.

Planned inclusions

  • Private safari 4×4 with pop-up roof and driver-guide
  • Park, conservation and vehicle access charges for the listed route
  • Listed game drives, picnic lunches and drinking water
  • One modest Karatu lodging night in shared twin/double rooms
  • Dinner on day 1, breakfast on day 2 and both picnic lunches
  • Ngorongoro crater descent charge

Not included

  • International and domestic flights
  • Airport transfers and hotels before or after the safari
  • Travel insurance, visa costs, tips and personal purchases
  • Unlisted activities and accommodation upgrades
