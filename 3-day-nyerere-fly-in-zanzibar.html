THE PLAN

Your itinerary

ZanzibarNyerere National ParkRufiji River
Day 1Zanzibar to the safari airstrip

Check in for the named scheduled flight with the confirmed baggage allowance. Meet your driver at the park airstrip, transfer to camp and join an afternoon game drive when flight arrival permits.

Day 2A wildlife day and a river perspective

Combine a game drive with the authorised boat safari at the timing chosen for the river conditions. Use life jackets and follow crew instructions. Meals and the second camp night are included in the agreed plan.

Day 3Return to Zanzibar

After breakfast, transfer to the airstrip for the scheduled flight back to Zanzibar. Allow a separate buffer before any onward international flight; hotel transport on the island can be added.

YOUR TRIP ARRANGEMENTS

What’s included

The itinerary below is the starting plan. Your written quote confirms the services for your dates and group.

Planned inclusions

  • Return scheduled Zanzibar–Nyerere flights for the agreed route, fare class and baggage allowance
  • Private park game-drive vehicle and driver-guide; camp/airstrip transfers
  • 2 tented-camp nights, two adults sharing
  • Meals from lunch on day 1 through breakfast on day 3; drinking water
  • Listed park, concession and vehicle charges, game drives and one authorised boat activity

Not included

  • International flights, visas and travel insurance
  • Unlisted meals, drinks, personal purchases and tips
  • Hotels and transfers before or after the listed itinerary
  • Zanzibar hotel–airport transfers unless added
  • Charter flights, extra baggage and walking safari
