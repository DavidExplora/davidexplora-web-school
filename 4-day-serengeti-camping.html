SAFARI · YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

Serengeti & Crater under canvas

Trade hotel walls for canvas and spend meaningful time in the Serengeti, ending with a crater game drive.

4 days · 3 nightsPrivate 4×4 · public campingArusha → Arusha

THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Picture yourself here.

SerengetiNgorongoroArusha
Day 1Through the highlands to the plains

Drive from Arusha through the Ngorongoro conservation area into the Serengeti. Enjoy a game drive when timing permits, then dinner at a public campsite.

Day 2A full Serengeti day

Follow a flexible game-drive plan with your guide. The day balances wildlife time, meals and suitable rest stops. Sleep in the Serengeti.

Day 3Serengeti morning, crater highlands evening

Take a morning game drive, then travel to the Ngorongoro highlands for a public campsite night.

Day 4Crater wildlife and return

Descend into the crater for a permitted game drive, enjoy a picnic lunch, then return to Arusha.

Planned inclusions

  • Private safari 4×4 with pop-up roof and driver-guide
  • Park, conservation and vehicle access charges for the listed route
  • Listed game drives, picnic lunches and drinking water
  • Three public campsite nights, shared tents and sleeping mats
  • Safari cook and meals from lunch on day 1 to lunch on day 4

Not included

  • Sleeping-bag rental unless specified in the quote

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

Public campsites have basic shared facilities and are not luxury camps. A migration-specific itinerary needs a seasonal route and separate quote.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
