THE PLAN

Your itinerary

TarangireSerengetiNgorongoroKaratu
Day 1Tarangire’s river country

Game drive in Tarangire, then continue to a modest Karatu lodge for dinner and the night.

Day 2Into the Serengeti

Drive through the highlands to the central Serengeti. Explore en route and settle into a lodge or tented camp.

Day 3Stay with the wildlife

Spend a full day exploring the central Serengeti with your guide and enjoy a second night in the same area.

Day 4Morning on the plains, evening in Karatu

Take a final Serengeti game drive and travel back through the highlands to Karatu.

Day 5The crater, then Arusha

Descend into Ngorongoro for a permitted game drive. After a picnic lunch, return to Arusha.
