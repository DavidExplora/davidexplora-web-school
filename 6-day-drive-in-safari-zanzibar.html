THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

ArushaTarangireSerengetiZanzibar
Day 1Tarangire and the highlands

Begin from the confirmed Arusha hotel in the morning. Spend time on a Tarangire game drive, then continue to the agreed Karatu lodge. Arrive in Arusha the previous day; that hotel night is quoted separately.

Day 2Across the highlands to the Serengeti

Travel through the permitted Ngorongoro transit route to the Serengeti, with a packed lunch and an afternoon game drive if arrival time allows. This is a substantial transfer day. Stay in the confirmed Serengeti tented camp.

Day 3A full Serengeti day

Explore the central Serengeti with your driver-guide, returning for the second camp night. Seasonal migration areas are considered only if the agreed route, permits and driving time support them.

Day 4A morning flight from the Serengeti to Zanzibar

Transfer to the named Serengeti airstrip after breakfast and take the confirmed scheduled flight to Zanzibar, which may have intermediate stops. Meet your island driver and continue to the named coast accommodation. Two island nights follow.

Day 5Time on the Zanzibar coast

Enjoy the confirmed coast guesthouse or hotel at your own pace. A Stone Town, forest or reef excursion can be quoted as an extra. This flexible day allows time to rest after the safari.

Day 6Departure or a longer island stay

Transfer to Zanzibar airport at the agreed time, or extend your coast stay with a separate quote. International flights are not included.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE PLANNING

What’s included

These are the planned inclusions for this itinerary. David confirms the exact services and total in your personal quote.

Planned inclusions

  • Private safari vehicle and English-speaking driver-guide on listed mainland days
  • 5 accommodation nights, two adults sharing; safari camps/lodges and two Zanzibar coast nights named in the quote
  • Full-board meals on the listed mainland safari days; breakfast on coast nights
  • Applicable park, transit, vehicle and concession charges for the confirmed safari route
  • One scheduled mainland-to-Zanzibar flight with the named ticket’s baggage allowance
  • Listed airstrip/airport transfers and drinking water on safari

Not included

  • International flights, visas and travel insurance
  • Unlisted meals, drinks, personal purchases and tips
  • Hotels and transfers before or after the listed itinerary
  • Zanzibar excursions, lunches and dinners unless specifically added
  • Additional safari nights or an extra crater day
  • Zanzibar visitor/accommodation levies not named in the quote

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

USD 3,400 is an off-peak estimate for two adults sharing. Arrive in Arusha before day 1; the pre-safari night is extra. The core six-day route visits Tarangire and the Serengeti, not a crater descent. Ngorongoro Crater or seasonal Ndutu can be added with a separately timed and priced route. The quote names the airline, luggage allowance, accommodation, visitor levies and actual services.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
