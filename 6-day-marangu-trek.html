THE PLAN

Your itinerary

MaranguMandaraHoromboKiboUhuru Peak

Time in Tanzania: allow 8 days including arrival and departure around the 6-day mountain route.

ArrivalSettle into Moshi

Check in for the first hotel night and meet the mountain team for a briefing and equipment check.

Mountain day 1Marangu Gate to Mandara

Register at the gate and walk through forest to Mandara Hut.

Mountain day 2Mandara to Horombo

Leave the forest for the moorland and continue to Horombo Hut.

Mountain day 3Acclimatisation around Horombo

Take the guide’s chosen acclimatisation walk and return to Horombo for another night.

Mountain day 4Horombo to Kibo

Continue across the high saddle to Kibo Hut, prepare for the summit attempt and rest.

Mountain day 5Summit attempt, then Horombo

Begin before dawn, attempt Uhuru if conditions and your guide allow, then descend to Horombo.

Mountain day 6Back through the forest

Descend to Marangu Gate and transfer to Moshi for the included post-trek hotel night.

DepartureLeave with a mountain story

Have breakfast and continue with your onward arrangements.

YOUR TRIP ARRANGEMENTS

What’s included

The itinerary below is the starting plan. Your written quote confirms the services for your dates and group.

Planned inclusions

  • Mountain guide, porter and cook team sized for your climb
  • Park, camping or hut and rescue charges for the mountain itinerary
  • Mountain meals and treated drinking water
  • Return transfers between Moshi and the route gates
  • Two Moshi hotel nights with breakfast: before and after the trek
  • Shared Marangu mountain hut accommodation

Not included

  • Flights and airport transfers
  • Travel insurance covering high-altitude trekking and evacuation
  • Tips for the mountain crew
  • Personal clothing, boots and sleeping-bag rental
  • Hotel lunches, dinners and additional hotel nights

GOOD TO KNOW

Practical details

The route days are mountain days. Allow the full trip length for arrival and departure. Acclimatisation, weather and the guide’s decisions affect the itinerary; reaching Uhuru is never guaranteed. Personal equipment, the crew plan and available safety equipment are confirmed before booking.

Tell me about food preferences, walking pace and access needs when you enquire. We’ll agree any changes to the route and price before you confirm.
