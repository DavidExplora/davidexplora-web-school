KILIMANJARO · YOUR NEXT CHAPTER

Machame: seven days above the clouds

Forest, moorland, Barranco and a high-camp night: a varied route with time built in for the climb.

7 mountain days · 9 trip daysMountain camping · 7-day routeMoshi → Moshi

THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Picture yourself here.

MachameShiraLava TowerBarrancoBarafuUhuru Peak

Time in Tanzania: allow 9 days including arrival and departure around the 7-day mountain route.

ArrivalMeet the team in Moshi

Use the included hotel night for your briefing, equipment check and rest.

Mountain day 1Forest to Machame Camp

Register at Machame Gate and follow the forest trail to camp.

Mountain day 2Above the trees to Shira

Walk into open moorland and continue to the Shira camping area.

Mountain day 3Lava Tower to Barranco

Follow the higher trail toward Lava Tower, then descend to Barranco for the night.

Mountain day 4Barranco Wall to Karanga

Cross Barranco Wall with your guide and continue to Karanga Camp.

Mountain day 5Karanga to Barafu

Move to high camp, prepare the summit plan and rest early.

Mountain day 6Uhuru attempt, then Mweka

Begin the summit attempt before dawn. If conditions allow, reach Uhuru, then descend to Mweka Camp.

Mountain day 7Forest descent and return

Walk to Mweka Gate and transfer to Moshi for your included post-trek hotel night.

DepartureOnward travel

Have breakfast and continue to your next destination.

Planned inclusions

  • Mountain guide, porter and cook team sized for your climb
  • Park, camping or hut and rescue charges for the mountain itinerary
  • Mountain meals and treated drinking water
  • Return transfers between Moshi and the route gates
  • Two Moshi hotel nights with breakfast: before and after the trek
  • Shared mountain tents and sleeping mats

Not included

  • Flights and airport transfers
  • Travel insurance covering high-altitude trekking and evacuation
  • Tips for the mountain crew
  • Personal clothing, boots and sleeping-bag rental
  • Hotel lunches, dinners and additional hotel nights

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

The route days are mountain days. Allow the full trip length for arrival and departure. Acclimatization, weather and the guide’s decisions affect the itinerary; reaching Uhuru is never guaranteed. Personal equipment, the crew plan and available safety equipment are confirmed before booking.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
