THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

Stone TownSpice farmJozaniZanzibar coast
Day 1Arrive and settle in Stone Town

Meet at Zanzibar Airport and transfer to the named Stone Town hotel. Keep arrival day flexible.

Day 2Stone Town with a local guide

Take a guided walking tour through public streets and the agreed heritage stops. Confirm any interior admission fees in advance; a Changuu boat visit is an optional addition.

Day 3Spices, then the coast

Visit the agreed spice farm with a local guide and tasting, then transfer to your named coastal stay. Choose the coast before quoting; the beach image illustrates Jambiani without fixing your hotel.

Day 4Jozani forest and the mangrove boardwalk

Take the guided forest and boardwalk visit with entry and transport specified. Wildlife is free-ranging; do not promise sightings or touch animals.

Day 5A day shaped by your chosen coast

Enjoy time at your hotel and beach. A Matemwe/Mnemba-water snorkeling trip or another boat day is optional, separately quoted and dependent on safe sea conditions.

Day 6An unhurried island day

Leave this day open for the beach, a local walk or a separately quoted activity. Tide times and conditions can change the order of island days.

Day 7Departure from Zanzibar Airport

Transfer to the airport for your flight, with enough time from the confirmed coastal hotel.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE PLANNING

What’s included

These are the planned inclusions for this itinerary. David confirms the exact services and total in your personal quote.

Planned inclusions

  • Six quoted hotel nights: Stone Town (2) and the chosen coast (4), with breakfast
  • Airport transfers and the Stone Town-to-coast transfer
  • Stone Town guide and explicitly listed admission fees
  • Spice-farm activity and tasting, Jozani guide/entry and scheduled activity transport

Not included

  • International or mainland-to-Zanzibar flights and any required entry documents/cover
  • Lunch, dinner, drinks and hotel extras unless specifically quoted
  • Optional Changuu, snorkeling, sunset boats or other activities
  • Tips, personal purchases and charity contributions

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

This is an individually quoted island plan, not a resort or room-category guarantee. The quote names the coast, properties, room basis, activity transport and admissions. Optional boat activities follow provider safety decisions and sea conditions. Any future education-support contribution would be separately agreed and documented.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
