## Before confirming a departure

1. Confirm the named guide and appropriate operating arrangements for the activity.
2. Agree the meeting point, hotel pickup area, adult/child numbers, start time, duration, access needs and dietary requirements.
3. Confirm transport, local hosts and activity/community fees. No unannounced home visits. Ask before photographing people.
4. Itemize the confirmed services and total. Museums, caves and waterfall additions to the Marangu village day are extras unless specifically included in the quote.
5. Confirm cancellation/weather handling, payment recipient and booking terms in writing.

The city walks meet in the relevant town. They do not include intercity transport. The transported village and nature-day estimates use standard Moshi pickup; Arusha, airport and distant-hotel transfers require their own quote.

Solo city estimates are $45 Arusha and $40 Moshi; Marangu solo is $135. These require separate cost confirmation. Do not divide a two-person quote automatically for a solo traveler.

For a target margin, use selling price = operating cost / (1 − target margin). At a 34% contribution target, divide the confirmed cost by 0.66, then by the actual paying party. If a channel takes commission, include it in the denominator alongside the margin and any sales deductions. For example, a 30% channel commission and 34% contribution target leave 36% of revenue for trip costs; do not simply copy the direct website price into that channel.
