## New idea to pilot with the host

Marangu Coffee Field Notes: an optional three-part learning activity using a guest's own notes or one reusable prompt card. Ask the host to explain a coffee-preparation step, practise three useful Swahili expressions with David, and let the guest record one thing learned about the visit. Suitable phrases are `Habari?` (How are you?), `Tafadhali` (Please) and `Asante` (Thank you).

This is a proposed service pilot, not a current advertised inclusion. First agree it with the host, confirm language and time needs, and rehearse the activity with one invited tester. Ask whether it improved the visit and what felt unnecessary. Add it to the public itinerary only after David confirms it can be delivered consistently, with any price change agreed. No app, certificate, paid tool or promised community contribution is required.
