## Market evidence and limits

[Mtango Safaris](https://www.mtango.co.tz/packages) lists a 2-day Mikumi trip from USD 475 with Morogoro/SGR pickup, a 3-day Nyerere road trip from USD 1,160, a Zanzibar–Nyerere fly-in trip from USD 1,330 and a 6-day safari/Zanzibar trip from USD 3,400. Check flight and island-accommodation inclusions carefully. These differ from the northern itineraries here; the prices cannot be copied as equivalent offers.

Operator benchmarks read on 11 October:

- [Haven Trails camping safari](https://www.haventrails.com/tanzania-camping-4-day-safari): published 2026–27 rates included USD 1,550 per adult for two and USD 1,159 for four. The four-adult proposal is close to this reference, pending actual costing.
- [Aardvark Expeditions mountain prices](https://www.aardvark-expeditions.com/kilimanjaro-national-park-tanzania/dates-prices/): examples included Marangu 6 days at USD 1,938, Machame 7 days at USD 2,169 and Lemosho 8 days at USD 2,451. Compare group, hotel and fee conditions before making a fixed offer.
- [Proud Zanzibar Tours](https://www.proudzanzibartours.com/price.html): examples included Stone Town USD 30, Prison Island USD 50 and Mnemba USD 70 for 2–6 travelers, with transfers excluded. David's estimates use their own stated inclusions, not that operator's services.

The official [Tanzania National Parks resources](https://www.tanzaniaparks.go.tz/) inspected still exposed a tariff PDF with an old validity period. It does not verify 2026 fees. Confirm current park, conservation, crater, vehicle, camping/hut and rescue charges directly; applicable taxes belong in the final payable total.

## Cost a real departure

Obtain written vehicle/driver/cook/crew costs, current permits, actual property and room/tent rates, meals, transfers, activity/boat fees and taxes. Divide shared costs over the real paying party, add per-person costs, then include David's operating margin and appropriate contingency. Do not price a couple as four travelers.

Name the contracting party, properties, exact services, date validity, payment method, cancellation terms and total in the quote. Children, residents, solo travelers, upgrades and flights need separate costing. Confirm the licensed operating arrangements for regulated activities rather than adding an unverified license claim.

Use a fixed dated offer only after costs and arrangements are confirmed. Update `data/tours.json`, regenerate, check and publish the matching source.

Day-trip competitive benchmarks, cost assumptions and modeled margins are in `DAYTRIP-OPERATIONS.md` and `data/daytrip-costs.json`. The master public estimates are in `data/tours.json`. Confirm actual supplier costs and applicable taxes before accepting payment.

New Zanzibar assumptions are in `ZANZIBAR-OPERATIONS.md`; new safari routes and screenshot price references are in `SAFARI-OPERATIONS.md`. Retail comparison prices do not establish supplier cost, availability or operating profit.
