Obtain actual aircraft fares and camp/vehicle/boat charges, current park/concession/vehicle/transit fees, meals, taxes and contingency. Set the final quote to retain the desired operating contribution after all direct costs; account separately for payment charges, distribution commissions, overhead and taxes. Reprice rather than promising an unprofitable departure at a screenshot benchmark.

## Boundaries that must survive every quote

- The Mikumi route is a two-day road trip from Dar es Salaam with one lodge night. Both days contain road time; it is not a fly-in day trip or two full game-drive days. Confirm park access periods and departure/return timing.
- Nyerere road travel and Nyerere fly-in travel have distinct departure and return arrangements. Fly-in airfare, camp availability, airstrip routing and baggage allowance require current confirmation. Do not promise a direct non-stop flight or a tight onward connection.
- The 6-day safari/island core has Tarangire day 1, Serengeti transfer day 2, full Serengeti day 3, island flight day 4, free coast day 5 and departure day 6. Nights: 1 Karatu, 2 Serengeti, 2 Zanzibar. Arrive in Arusha before day 1; that overnight is separate. A crater descent or Ndutu routing needs its own time/price, not an impossible transfer inserted into the six-day plan.
- The 6-day northern culture route includes a host-agreed visit near the highlands, not an uninvited community or school visit. Name the host, fee, activity, consent/photography approach and language arrangement.
- The 4-day luxury route uses Tarangire, Manyara and Ngorongoro. Luxury means the named properties and room categories in the quote; the Serengeti is a longer/flight extension.
- Boating and walking operate only under the appropriate authorised provider/permit arrangements. Boat conditions vary; walking is not silently included. Wildlife and migration crossings are not guaranteed.
