## Cost model

Editable assumptions: `data/zanzibar-costs.json`. Each model is for the catalogue’s stated adult group. Sum all non-group values, then subtract from `tour.price × group`. Modeled direct operating contribution is at least 34% before business overhead, tax treatment, card/payment fees and distribution commission. This is not guaranteed net profit.

A 30% OTA commission is not included in these direct-website models. Reprice OTA listings separately; never assume a website margin survives distribution fees.

For each departure, obtain a written supplier cost and confirm current admission/marine charges, boat capacity, life jackets, instructors, eligibility, cancellation/weather terms and transfer costs. For jet ski, disclose age/licence restrictions, damage excess, deposit and insurance terms before payment. For diving, the provider controls medical clearance and programme limits; disclose failed-clearance/refund terms.

Six-day stays: verify both named guesthouses, room basis, five breakfasts, all transfer legs and the ocean picnic; itemise local visitor/accommodation levies and mandatory insurance arrangements. The USD 690 estimate cannot be represented as a held room or all-inclusive resort.
