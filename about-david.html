WHERE THE IDEA STARTS

Local knowledge,
with a clear plan.

David Explora starts with a conversation. You tell me what you want from your time in Tanzania, and I help turn that into a route with the right number of days, clear arrangements and a price you can understand.

I grew up in the Marangu region, where the mountain and everyday village life meet. Guiding has taught me to listen to a traveller’s pace and explain what the day involves. My tourism management and marketing studies help me build that practical experience into a better-planned business. David Explora is a young venture I am developing with care, one well-organised journey at a time.

I speak English and Kiswahili and am developing my Spanish. If you need a Spanish-speaking guide or individual support, tell me early so the actual arrangements can be checked for your dates. Each written itinerary explains who guides the activities and how the transfers, accommodation and costs fit together.
