THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

MichamviBlue LagoonSnorkeling
MeetStart on the Michamvi coast

Meet at the named launch and check the day’s tide window. Fit your equipment, discuss swimming ability and hear the boat briefing.

SnorkelFollow the guide in suitable water

Visit the permitted area that suits the day’s visibility and conditions. Do not touch coral, stand on reef or handle marine life; photography never requires lifting animals.

ReturnA relaxed return to the shore

Have seasonal fruit and drinking water before returning to the same launch. An optional lunch or hotel transfer can be priced separately.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE PLANNING

What’s included

These are the planned inclusions for this itinerary. David confirms the exact services and total in your personal quote.

Planned inclusions

  • Shared local boat, crew and life jackets
  • Snorkeling mask, fins, guide and buoyancy support
  • Applicable marine access charges, fruit and drinking water

Not included

  • Hotel transfers and accommodation
  • Travel insurance, tips and personal purchases
  • Activities or meals not listed

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

The starting estimate uses four adults joining a shared boat and excludes hotel transport. Blue Lagoon is a Michamvi-coast excursion, separate from Safari Blue in Menai Bay and the private Mnemba island. Water clarity, marine life and tides vary.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
