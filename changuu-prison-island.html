THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

ChanguuPrison IslandStone Town
DepartLeave the Stone Town seafront

Meet your guide, receive the boat briefing and cross when conditions permit.

VisitHistory and tortoise conservation

Walk the heritage areas and observe the tortoises with the local guide. Follow sanctuary rules and give animals space; no handling or staged animal photographs are promised.

ReturnCross back to Stone Town

Return by boat at the agreed time. Snorkeling is not part of this itinerary unless specifically quoted.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE PLANNING

What’s included

These are the planned inclusions for this itinerary. David confirms the exact services and total in your personal quote.

Planned inclusions

  • Shared return boat crossing from Stone Town, life jacket and local guide
  • Island entry and listed sanctuary/conservation charges
  • Drinking water

Not included

  • Hotel transfers and accommodation
  • Meals, snorkeling and unlisted activities
  • Insurance, personal purchases and tips

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

Meet on the Stone Town seafront. Island entry and listed sanctuary charges are included in the estimate; hotel transfers are extra. Do not touch, lift or feed tortoises except under an authorised sanctuary protocol. Sea conditions may change the departure. Boat size, safety equipment and capacity are confirmed before payment.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
