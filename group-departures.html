FROM INTEREST TO A CONFIRMED PLAN

A group journey,
with clear arrangements.

These are departure proposals. No seats, accommodation or park services are reserved by this webpage, and an enquiry does not commit you to a payment.

  1. Tell David about your party

    Send your chosen departure, arrival plan, language request and any support needs. One traveller can enquire about joining; the final party must be viable.

  2. Review the written proposal

    David confirms the minimum operating group, guides, licensed operating arrangements, hotels or camps, inclusions, full price and the decision deadline.

  3. Confirm the terms and payment route

    Review cancellation terms and what happens if the group cannot run. Follow verified payment instructions and keep your written booking confirmation before buying non-refundable flights.
