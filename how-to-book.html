How to book with David Explora.

Send your trip details, review a personal itinerary and quote, then agree the arrangements in writing.

01 / YOUR TRIP DETAILS

Send your request.

Share your dates, group size, budget and preferred activities. The form prepares a WhatsApp or email draft; press Send in that app to deliver it.

Prepare my request ↗

02 / ITINERARY & PRICE

Review your written quote.

Check accommodation, transfers, activities, meals, fees and the total. The quote explains what is included, what is paid separately and the applicable cancellation terms.

03 / PAYMENT ARRANGEMENTS

Agree before you pay.

Confirm the contracting party, payment recipient, currency, deposit and balance deadline. This website does not currently offer online checkout or reserve a place automatically.

04 / BEFORE DEPARTURE

Keep your confirmation.

Keep your receipt and written booking confirmation. Confirm the pickup point, contact details, packing guidance and any outstanding balance.

Booking questions

Is the displayed price final?

No. Prices are estimates based on the stated adult group size. Your written quote confirms the total for your dates, group and services.

Can I combine a safari, trek and Zanzibar?

Yes. Ask for a combined itinerary with time for preparation, recovery and transfers.

How do I know my enquiry arrived?

Send the prepared draft in WhatsApp or your email app, then wait for David’s reply. The website cannot confirm message delivery.
