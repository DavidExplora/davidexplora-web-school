PERSONAL JOURNEYS · TANZANIA

A conversation. A clear quote. Your journey.

Plan directly with David, review the arrangements and confirm your trip in writing. Your inquiry is the first step.

01 / START WITH YOUR IDEA

Tell David what matters.

Choose a journey or share your dates, group size, budget and interests. Prepare your request on WhatsApp or email, then press Send in that app.

Prepare my request ↗

02 / KNOW WHAT YOU ARE BOOKING

Review your written quote.

Confirm the itinerary, accommodation, transport, activities, inclusions, exclusions and total price. Ask which fees and transfers are included and how changes or cancellations are handled.

03 / AGREE THE PAYMENT

Pay against a confirmed quote.

Ask David for the available payment method, currency, deposit amount, balance deadline and any processing charges in writing. Online checkout is not currently available on this website. A trip request does not collect money or reserve a place.

04 / KEEP YOUR CONFIRMATION

Get the details before departure.

Keep your payment receipt and written booking confirmation. Confirm pickup, contact details, packing guidance and any outstanding balance before traveling.

Questions worth asking.

Is the guide price my final price?

No. Guide prices are estimates with group-size assumptions shown on each itinerary. Dates, availability, accommodation and transfers affect your personal quote.

Can I combine a safari with Kilimanjaro or Zanzibar?

Yes, ask for a combined itinerary. Include preparation, recovery and transfers rather than counting only activity days.

How do I know my request arrived?

The website prepares a draft. Send it in WhatsApp or your email app and wait for David’s reply. Copy the message if your email app does not open.
