BEFORE YOU GO

A few good questions.

How do I book with David Explora?

Start with the request form or a WhatsApp conversation. David will discuss your dates and interests, then send a proposed itinerary and a written quote. Your trip is confirmed only after you agree to the arrangements and booking terms and receive written confirmation.

Can I combine Kilimanjaro, safari and Zanzibar?

Yes, you can request a combined itinerary. Allow enough time for the mountain route, recovery, mainland travel and the Zanzibar transfer. David will help you choose a realistic order and pace.

Are prices and availability confirmed on this website?

Each trip is quoted personally. Costs depend on the dates, group size, route, accommodation, park fees and transport. Website itineraries are illustrative; the written quote sets out the confirmed arrangements and inclusions.

Can I travel privately or join a small group?

Ask about either option. Private arrangements can be shaped around your group. Shared departures depend on matching dates and availability and will be confirmed directly.

Can I visit Kilimanjaro without a summit trek?

Yes. A guided day hike or a local experience around the foothills can suit a shorter visit. Summit treks need a suitable multi-day route and extra time for arrival and departure.
