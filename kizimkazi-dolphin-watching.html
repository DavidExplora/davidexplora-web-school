Kizimkazi Dolphin Watching & Coastal Morning

Explore Zanzibar’s south coast by boat and look for wild dolphins from a respectful distance. A quiet coastal morning, with no promised sighting or dolphin swim.

THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

KizimkaziSouth coastWild dolphins
MeetAn early coastal start

Meet at the agreed Kizimkazi landing point for a crew briefing, weather check and life-jacket fitting.

At seaObserve without chasing

The crew searches at a measured pace and watches from a distance. No chasing, blocking, touching, feeding or placing swimmers around dolphins is part of this itinerary. Leave the area if animals show avoidance.

ReturnBack to the village shore

Return to the departure shore after the agreed boat time. The final route and timing depend on sea conditions and wildlife behaviour.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed. This is boat-based observation, not swimming with dolphins. Only confirm once the skipper agrees to the no-chasing approach and suitable safety arrangements. Transfers from north-coast hotels are extra and require an early start.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
