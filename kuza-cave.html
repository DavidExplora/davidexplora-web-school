THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

Kuza CaveJambianiSwahili heritage
ArrivalMeet at the culture centre

Meet at Kuza Cave at the confirmed time and hear the local guidance on respectful visiting and the cave access.

CaveA freshwater pause

Follow the steps down to the cave. Swimming is optional; water depth, visibility and safe access must be assessed on the day. Bring suitable footwear.

CultureTime at the heritage displays

Explore the included Swahili heritage information and finish at the centre. Cooking, drumming lessons and lunch are optional additions, not part of this short visit.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

The duration excludes transport. Cave access is via uneven, sometimes slippery steps and is not step-free. Swimming is optional; non-swimmers should not enter the cave water. Children need adult supervision. Tell David about swimming ability and mobility needs before the visit. The photographs show the nearby Jambiani coast rather than the cave interior.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
