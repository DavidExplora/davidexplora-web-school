A DIFFERENT MARANGU DAY

Walk the rainforest.
Return to Moshi that evening.

Choose the national park day hike from Marangu Gate towards the Mandara area. Walk at your group’s pace with a mountain guide; the permitted turnaround and weather determine how far you go.

This is a separate mountain experience. The village day does not include park access, and the forest hike is not a summit climb.

USD 245 / adult Estimate for 2 adults · return Moshi transport, mountain guide, applicable day-access fees, picnic lunch and water.
