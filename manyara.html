WHY LAKE MANYARA

A small park with changing landscapes.

Lake Manyara National Park brings together groundwater forests, open areas, wetlands and the lake itself, creating a different landscape experience within northern Tanzania.

Its location also makes it a useful part of a wider northern safari journey, particularly when combined with destinations such as Tarangire and Ngorongoro.

TRAVEL WITH RESPECT

Protecting the park is part of the journey.

Lake Manyara is a protected wildlife area. Responsible travel means respecting park rules, wildlife, other visitors and the natural environment.

Wildlife viewing and activities can depend on official regulations, seasonal conditions and local operating requirements.
