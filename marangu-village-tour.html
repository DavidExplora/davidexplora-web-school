THE PLAN

Your itinerary

MaranguChagga cultureCoffeeKilimanjaro foothills
MorningTravel from Moshi to Marangu

Pickup from an agreed Moshi hotel and transfer to Marangu. Meet the local guide and hosts; confirm the walking route and the day’s arrangements.

VillageA host-led village and farming walk

Walk through public village paths and host-approved farming areas. Discuss banana and coffee cultivation, family life and Chagga traditions. Visits to homes are by invitation, never unannounced.

CoffeeCoffee preparation with local hosts

Learn how coffee is prepared and join the agreed roasting or grinding activity. The venue and host are confirmed before departure; activities may vary with local arrangements.

LunchA local meal, then return to Moshi

Have the planned local lunch, with dietary requirements discussed beforehand. Return to Moshi in the afternoon. Waterfalls, caves or museums can be requested as separately quoted additions.

YOUR TRIP ARRANGEMENTS

GOOD TO KNOW

Practical details

This is a village and cultural day, not a Kilimanjaro National Park trek. The photographed village scenery illustrates Marangu, not a promised private homestead. Ask permission before photographing people. The route has uneven paths; share access and dietary needs. Hosts and final arrangements must be confirmed before the tour.

Tell me about food preferences, walking pace and access needs when you enquire. We’ll agree any changes to the route and price before you confirm.
