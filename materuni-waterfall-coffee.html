THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

MateruniMoshiCoffeeWaterfall
MorningFrom Moshi into the foothills

Transfer to Materuni and meet the local guide and hosts.

Late morningWalk to the waterfall

Follow the guided trail at your group’s pace. Mud, rain and access conditions affect the walk.

AfternoonCoffee, lunch and the return

Explore the coffee process with local hosts, have lunch and return to Moshi.
