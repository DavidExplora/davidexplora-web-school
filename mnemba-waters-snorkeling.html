GOOD TO KNOW

Practical details

There is no Mnemba island landing and no guaranteed dolphin sighting. Activities depend on sea conditions. Confirm swimming ability, safety equipment and any access needs before booking.

Tell me about food preferences, walking pace and access needs when you enquire. We’ll agree any changes to the route and price before you confirm.
