GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

There is no Mnemba island landing and no guaranteed dolphin sighting. Activities depend on sea conditions. Confirm swimming ability, safety equipment and any access needs before booking.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
