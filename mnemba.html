MNEMBA WATERS · ZANZIBAR

Follow the blue.Leave room for the ocean.

Explore the waters around Mnemba on a snorkeling day shaped around the sea conditions and your comfort in the water.

Plan this experience ↗ Explore more

A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE

A marine day, with the details considered.

The waters around Mnemba are known for snorkeling and marine scenery. This is a boat excursion in the surrounding waters; landing on the private island or visiting its resort is not included.

YOUR DAY, YOUR WAY

What could your day hold?

01

Your coastal start

Plan a pickup and transfer based on where you are staying. The departure point, boat and time on the water are confirmed before booking.

02

Time in the water

Discuss your swimming ability and the equipment you need. Snorkeling plans depend on visibility, weather and the guide’s assessment of conditions.

03

Respect the reef

Keep your distance from marine life and avoid touching or standing on coral. Wildlife encounters are natural and cannot be guaranteed.

GOOD TO KNOW

Plan the practical details.

Usually planned as a day excursion with transfers. David will confirm the current access rules, marine fees, boat arrangements and inclusions. No dolphin encounter is promised, and the private island is outside this excursion.
