THE PLAN

Your itinerary

MoshiTown centreLocal marketRailway area
MeetMeet in central Moshi

Meet at Moshi Clock Tower, or another agreed central meeting point. Discuss your interests and learn a few Swahili greetings before setting off.

MarketFood, produce and everyday trade

Walk through a working central market to discover produce and daily trade. Your guide helps you understand local buying habits without pressuring you to shop.

WalkMoshi streets and railway heritage

Explore public streets and suitable public viewing points around the old railway area. Entry to railway facilities or tracks is not included; follow your guide’s directions.

CoffeeStop for tea or coffee

Enjoy tea or coffee and one local snack at an agreed local stop. Finish in central Moshi with recommendations for the rest of your stay.

YOUR TRIP ARRANGEMENTS

What’s included

The itinerary below is the starting plan. Your written quote confirms the services for your dates and group.

Planned inclusions

  • Local English-speaking walking guide
  • Guided market and public-street route
  • Tea or coffee, one local snack and drinking water
  • Local recommendations and a short Swahili introduction

Not included

  • Hotel pickup and transport from Arusha or the airport
  • Paid museum or railway-facility entry
  • Lunch, shopping, extra drinks and tips

GOOD TO KNOW

Practical details

This tour takes place in Moshi, not Arusha. Walking surfaces can be uneven and markets busy. Confirm dietary needs and walking limitations in advance. Kilimanjaro views depend on cloud and visibility; no mountain view is promised.

Tell me about food preferences, walking pace and access needs when you enquire. We’ll agree any changes to the route and price before you confirm.
