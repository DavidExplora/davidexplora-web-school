WHY NGORONGORO

A crater is only the beginning.

The Ngorongoro Conservation Area brings together dramatic volcanic landscapes, wildlife, cultural heritage and places connected to the story of human evolution.

Your journey can focus on the famous crater, but it can also be shaped around photography, landscapes, culture, walking experiences or a wider northern Tanzania safari.

EXPERIENCE NGORONGORO

Choose what you want to discover.

Different travelers experience Ngorongoro in different ways. We build the journey around your interests and available time.

01

Crater Wildlife

Explore the crater floor and its varied habitats while looking for elephants, buffaloes, lions, hippos, wildebeest, zebras and other wildlife.

02

Volcanic Landscapes

Experience the dramatic relationship between crater rims, highlands, forests, grasslands and volcanic landscapes that give the area its distinctive character.

03

Culture & Heritage

Discover the cultural and historical dimensions of the conservation area through designated experiences and opportunities to learn about local communities.

04

Photography & Nature

Capture crater landscapes, wildlife, birdlife and changing light while travelling with respect for conservation rules and the surrounding environment.

TRAVEL WITH RESPECT

Conservation is part of the journey.

Ngorongoro is a protected conservation area where wildlife, natural landscapes, cultural heritage and communities are part of the wider story. Our journeys should respect the area's rules, people and environment.

Activities and access can depend on official regulations, permits, local conditions and the requirements of the conservation authority.
