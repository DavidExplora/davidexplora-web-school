CURRENT BOOKING PROCESS

Pay against an agreed quote.

Instant card checkout is not active on this website. This page prepares a request for instructions; it does not charge a card, collect payment details or confirm a reservation.

  1. Agree your travel plan

    Check the dates, named operating arrangements, transport, hotels or camps, inclusions, full price and booking terms.

  2. Receive verified instructions

    Ask David for the payment recipient, amount, currency and method tied to your quote. If a card payment link is provided, enter card details only on the approved provider’s secure checkout.

  3. Check the recipient

    For mobile money, check the merchant name on your provider’s own confirmation screen. Contact David if it differs from the written instructions. Your PIN stays in your provider’s app or phone menu.

  4. Keep written confirmation

    A payment receipt records a payment. David’s written booking confirmation records the agreed tour, dates and services. Keep both.
