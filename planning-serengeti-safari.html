DAVID EXPLORA · SAFARI

Give the Serengeti enough time: a northern safari that works

A Serengeti safari needs time for both game drives and the road journey. Start with your arrival point, the nights in each area and where you’ll go afterwards. This guide explains the choices behind a northern Tanzania itinerary.

David Explora planning notes · 13 October 2026 · 2 min read

Your starting point changes the route

Arusha is a common starting point for the northern circuit. A Moshi pickup, late international arrival or flight from Zanzibar changes the first day. Tell David where you will actually be staying and when you can leave.

TANAPA’s Serengeti visitor guide describes a substantial road journey from Arusha. Treat any published driving time as a planning estimate. Road conditions, park procedures and stops determine your real day.

Short trips need a smaller footprint

If you only have a day, a suitable Tarangire itinerary can be a clearer choice than trying to force the Serengeti into a road day. A two-day Tarangire and Ngorongoro route has its own departure and overnight requirements.

A four- or five-day northern safari can give the Serengeti a meaningful place in the plan. Check how many nights are actually inside or near the park, when game drives happen and which transfer days reduce wildlife time.

Match the accommodation to the wildlife plan

A camp name alone does not tell you whether it suits the area you hope to explore. Ask where it is, what the next day’s driving involves and whether the quoted itinerary follows the seasonal region you are discussing.

A northern river-crossing itinerary may need additional nights beyond the standard central Serengeti route. A crossing, Big Five checklist or exact sighting cannot be guaranteed. Choose the route for the opportunity and landscape, then let the guide work with the conditions.

Keep onward travel visible

A safari-and-Zanzibar package should state the flight, baggage rules, airport or airstrip transfers and island hotel location. The coast is not simply a photograph added to the end of a safari card.

Before confirming, request a day-by-day plan with every overnight and transfer shown. It is easier to adjust a realistic route before booking than to repair a rushed one on the road.
