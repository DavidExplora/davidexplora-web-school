Stone Town, the departure point for this experience

A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE

An island afternoon, thoughtfully planned.

Often called Prison Island, Changuu is a popular boat excursion from Stone Town. Its heritage buildings and tortoise sanctuary offer a different side of Zanzibar, and the visit can be combined with a Stone Town walk.

YOUR DAY, YOUR WAY

What could your day hold?

01

The crossing

Enjoy a coastal boat journey from Stone Town. Departure times and the crossing depend on the weather and the boat arrangements.

02

Island heritage

Explore the island’s historic setting with a guide. Ask about access and any entry fees when the day is planned.

03

Tortoise sanctuary

Visit the giant tortoises with care. Follow the sanctuary staff’s instructions and keep animal welfare at the center of your visit.

GOOD TO KNOW

Plan the practical details.

A half-day idea, with exact timing confirmed for your hotel and dates. Boat transport, transfers, guide and entry charges will be itemized in your quote. Swimming or snorkeling is subject to suitable conditions.
