Privacy information

Who you are contacting

David Explora is David Amani Lyamuya's Tanzania travel brand. For questions about your information, email davidlyamuya819@gmail.com or contact +255 778 321 400 on WhatsApp.

What the request form does

The form uses the details you enter to prepare a WhatsApp or email draft in your browser. It does not submit those details to a David Explora website database. David receives your inquiry only when you send the message through WhatsApp, or email him directly. Please do not include passport details, payment card information or other sensitive documents in an initial inquiry.

How inquiry details are used

When you contact David, he uses your contact details, selected itinerary, optional budget, travel preferences and dates to respond, discuss arrangements and prepare a quote. If you proceed with a trip, he may need additional information and may share relevant details with the guides, accommodation or transport providers involved. Ask what information is needed before sending sensitive documents.

Third-party services

WhatsApp, email providers, social platforms and the website host process information under their own policies. Opening WhatsApp transfers the prepared message to that service, including the details you chose to provide. Website hosting may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the site.

Browser storage and analytics

The website's planner and inquiry form do not use cookies or browser storage to remember your details. No advertising or analytics trackers have been added to this website. Hosting and third-party services may have their own technical storage or logging.

Your choices

You can stop before sending a prepared message. You can also contact David to ask about the inquiry information he holds, request a correction or request deletion. Information needed for a confirmed trip, accounting or applicable legal obligations may need to be retained; David will explain when that applies.
