YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy information

Updated 15 October 2026

Who you are contacting

David Explora is David Amani Lyamuya's Tanzania travel brand. For questions about your information, email info@davidexplora.com or contact +255 778 321 400 on WhatsApp.

What the request form does

When you choose Send enquiry by email in the main trip form, your name, email address, optional WhatsApp number, trip preferences and message are submitted to FormSubmit for email delivery to info@davidexplora.com. Complete its anti-spam check to submit the enquiry. FormSubmit’s documentation states that it retains submissions for 30 days. This website does not create a reservation database or store these details in browser storage.

The WhatsApp option prepares a message in your browser; you review and send it in WhatsApp. Direct email links open your email app. Please do not include passport details, payment card information, medical records or other sensitive documents in an initial enquiry.

How inquiry details are used

When you contact David, he uses your contact details, selected itinerary, optional budget, travel preferences, starting location, dates and date flexibility to respond, discuss arrangements and prepare a quote. If you proceed with a trip, he may need additional information and may share relevant details with the guides, accommodation or transport providers involved. Ask what information is needed before sending sensitive documents.

Promotion links and enquiry sources

If you arrive through a labelled promotion link, the website remembers its source, medium, campaign label and entry page in this browser tab for up to 30 minutes. The entry page excludes query strings and fragments. These labels help David understand which promotion led to an enquiry; they are included only when you submit the main enquiry form or send its prepared message. The optional “How did you find David Explora?” answer is included separately. This feature does not send visits to an analytics service, create a visitor identifier or save your contact details in browser storage. You can leave the optional answer blank, close the tab or contact David directly.

Optional Google Analytics

The website offers a choice to allow or decline Google Analytics 4 cookies. Its tag loads only after you choose to allow it. It measures page visits, use of the email and WhatsApp contact buttons, and attempts to submit the main enquiry form. These events use the page, source labels and contact method; enquiry text, names, email addresses and telephone numbers are excluded from these custom event fields. An enquiry attempt does not confirm delivery, payment or a booking.

Google processes permitted Analytics data under its privacy policy. The integration keeps advertising storage, advertising user data, advertising personalisation and Google signals disabled. The website remembers your Analytics choice in this browser for up to 180 days. You can reopen Analytics choices in the footer, decline collection and remove Analytics cookies set on this domain. A browser-storage failure leaves collection off until you make a choice on that page.

Third-party enquiry services

FormSubmit processes main-form email enquiries and provides an anti-spam check, which may use Google reCAPTCHA. Read FormSubmit’s privacy information, its submission retention documentation and Google’s privacy policy. You can choose direct WhatsApp or email contact instead of the form delivery service.

WhatsApp, email providers, social platforms and the website host process information under their own policies. Opening WhatsApp transfers the prepared message to that service, including the details you chose to provide. Website hosting may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to serve and secure the site.

Browser storage and live chat

The planner and inquiry form do not store your entered personal details in browser storage. If you save a tour, its catalogue ID is stored in this browser’s local storage so your shortlist is available on your next visit. Saved article IDs are also stored locally when you use the journal’s reading list. These lists are not sent to David or a public profile. Remove a tour or guide with its Saved button, or clear this website’s browser data to erase both lists. If storage is blocked, saving lasts only for the current visit. Optional Analytics uses the choices described above. Live chat uses the separate service described below. Destination films are served by this website; opening social links uses the selected platform. Hosting and third-party services may have their own technical logging.

Live chat with David

The chat widget is provided by tawk.to. It lets David see active visitor sessions and respond to messages. When the widget loads, tawk.to may process your IP address, browser and device information, pages visited and session activity. It may use cookies or browser storage to maintain the chat session, subject to the widget’s consent settings.

If you choose to chat, tawk.to processes and stores your messages and any contact details you provide so David can respond to your enquiry. The website does not automatically copy details from the trip planner or individual travel support forms into chat. Please keep payment card details, passwords, passport copies and medical records out of chat.

See tawk.to’s privacy policy for its data handling and privacy options. You can also contact David through the WhatsApp and email links. Ask David if you want to discuss, correct or delete information you have shared in a conversation.

Education partnership enquiries

The education page opens a WhatsApp or email draft for a partnership conversation. It does not collect donations, create a subscriber list or request student records. Please keep students’ names, photographs and private information out of an initial enquiry.

Your choices

You can stop before sending a prepared message. You can also contact David to ask about the inquiry information he holds, request a correction or request deletion. Information needed for a confirmed trip, accounting or applicable legal obligations may need to be retained; David will explain when that applies.

Voluntary support enquiries and payment requests

The English and Spanish support forms prepare a message locally in your browser. You choose which functional assistance details to include and review the draft before sending it through WhatsApp or your email app. The website does not upload or store these form details. Do not submit diagnoses, medical records, passport copies, card numbers, CVVs, PINs or banking passwords.

If you send the message, David receives the information through the service you chose. Those services process it under their own privacy terms. Support details are used to discuss your itinerary; agree which practical requirements may be shared with an accommodation, transport or guiding provider before arrangements are made.

The payment-request form only prepares a request for verified instructions using your quote reference. It does not collect card details or take payment. Any future card payment link uses the approved provider’s checkout and privacy terms.

The welcome prompt uses session-only display and dismissal flags on this browser. It contains no personal information and is not used for advertising or tracking.
