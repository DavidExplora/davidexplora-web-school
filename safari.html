THE SERENGETI, BY SEASON

Plan for wildlife.
Leave room for nature.

Migration routes and timing vary with rainfall and grazing. Southern Serengeti/Ndutu can suit a calving-season enquiry; northern Serengeti plans require the right camps and enough road or flight time. A crossing or a specific animal sighting is never guaranteed.

Private vehicle

Agree who shares the vehicle, luggage capacity and actual transfer arrangements. Your quote explains the vehicle and any shared flights or boat activities.

Share ages, room preferences, mobility needs and dietary requirements. Camp age rules and optional activities need confirmation before quoting.

Comfort & camp choice

Compare camping, lodges and tented camps with named properties and room categories. Decide what matters to you rather than relying on a general luxury label.

How to read the prices Guide prices are estimates in USD per international adult for the group and sharing arrangement shown. Your dates, accommodation, transfers and fees determine the final total. Children, residents and other group sizes receive their own quote. David’s written quote lists the full price, inclusions, extras and booking terms before you confirm.
