WHY SERENGETI

A landscape built for discovery.

The Serengeti is one of Tanzania's defining wildlife landscapes, known for its open plains and rich wildlife. But the experience is not only about ticking animals off a list.

Your journey can be shaped around the amount of time you have, the areas you want to explore and the kind of safari experience you want to create.
