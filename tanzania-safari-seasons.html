Safari seasons: choose the landscape, then plan the camps

There is no single month that suits every Tanzania trip. Your priorities might be open-plains wildlife, birdlife, quieter camps or a combination with the coast. Start with those priorities and your dates, then discuss where to stay.

David Explora planning notes · 13 October 2026 · 2 min read

June to October: often drier safari conditions

TANAPA describes June–October as the main dry season. Wildlife can gather around available water, making the location of your park visits relevant. Popular dates also need early accommodation planning.

Do not assume a central Serengeti stay and a northern migration plan are the same itinerary. Ask which region your guide recommends for your dates and how many transfer days that choice adds.

Rain changes the planning conversation

November–December is generally associated with short rains, while March–May brings the longer rainy period. Green landscapes can be rewarding, but road access and lodge operation need checking for the actual route.

Rainfall is variable. A seasonal label is not a weather forecast for your departure. Build enough flexibility for a guide to adjust the game-drive order or an operator to propose a more suitable overnight.

Let wildlife remain wildlife

Seasonal wildlife travel should be presented as an informed opportunity, not a promised event. A guide can read the landscape, use local information and decide where to spend time. Animals do not follow a booking confirmation.

The same applies to a specific animal checklist. Choose an itinerary you would value for its habitats, guiding and time outdoors, even when a hoped-for moment does not happen.

Ask for the seasonal details in your quote

For a combined trip, check the mountain or island plan separately. A general northern safari season should not be treated as a guarantee of mountain weather or safe boat conditions.

  • Which Serengeti region and camp location fit the proposed dates?
  • Are the named stays operating, and which room or tent basis is included?
  • What changes if a road, activity or sea connection is unsuitable?
  • How do the dates affect the complete price for your actual group?
