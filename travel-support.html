A CONVERSATION BEFORE A PROMISE

Plan the details that matter to you.

This enquiry is for travellers with disabilities, reduced mobility, limb differences or other assistance needs, and for their companions. Choose the arrangements that matter to you; no diagnosis or medical documents are requested.

Check the actual place and activity

City visits, vehicle-based safaris and coastal stays can be discussed, with alternatives to long walks where suitable. Steps, vehicle boarding, bathroom layouts, path surfaces and boat access are checked with the specific provider.

The catalogue is not a list of certified accessible tours. Mountain trails and forest walks can be steep and uneven; we do not promise that every route is suitable or that specialist assistance is available. The written proposal states what has been checked, what remains difficult and any support costs.

Spanish, with clear language arrangements

David speaks English and Kiswahili and is developing his Spanish. You can send this enquiry in Spanish. A Spanish-speaking guide is a request that must be confirmed for your dates; this page does not guarantee guide availability.

Agree the support before you book

We put the agreed assistance, provider, accommodation, vehicle and cost in writing. Tell David whether a companion travels with you and which tasks you would like support with. Share only what is needed to plan the trip.
