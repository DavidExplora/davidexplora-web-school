GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

The USD 85 estimate is per participating adult using one craft, based on two adults booking separate craft. Age, licence, weight, deposit and insurance/excess requirements are provider-specific and must be disclosed before payment. Ask for the exact written damage-liability terms. This is not a 60-minute session or wildlife tour.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
