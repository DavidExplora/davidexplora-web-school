THE JOURNEY, DAY BY DAY

Your itinerary

Zanzibar mangrovesTidal channelsCoastal ecology
BriefingMeet at the confirmed launch

Your quote names the launch site, meeting pin and tide window. Meet the guide, fit your buoyancy aid and practise basic paddling.

PaddleFollow the tidal channels

Follow the guide through suitable channels without disturbing roots or wildlife. The route changes with water levels, wind and your group’s ability.

FinishReturn with the tide

Return to the same launch, with time for water and questions. Avoid stepping on mangrove roots or leaving anything in the water.

KNOW WHAT YOU ARE PLANNING

What’s included

These are the planned inclusions for this itinerary. David confirms the exact services and total in your personal quote.

Planned inclusions

  • Standard kayak, paddle, buoyancy aid and local guide
  • Drinking water and listed launch/community charges

Not included

  • Hotel transfers and accommodation
  • Travel insurance, tips and personal purchases
  • Activities or meals not listed
  • Transparent kayak upgrade (estimated from USD 60 per adult; separate availability)

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you go.

USD 45 is for a standard kayak. A clear/transparent kayak is an optional equipment upgrade, not guaranteed by this price. The final quote names the venue, kayak type and route. Share swimming ability and access needs; strong wind or unsuitable tides can postpone the activity.

Share your dates, actual group size, accommodation preference and access or dietary needs. Changes to the route and any extra costs are agreed with you in writing.
