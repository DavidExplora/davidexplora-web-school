BEYOND THE BEACH

Zanzibar is an island of stories.

Zanzibar can be a place to slow down by the ocean, but the island also carries layers of history, culture, coastal life and marine experiences.

At David Explora, we want your Zanzibar experience to reflect what actually interests you. You might begin with the streets of Stone Town, continue to an island excursion or spend time exploring the marine world around Mnemba.

And Zanzibar does not have to stand alone. It can become the island chapter of a larger Tanzania journey alongside Kilimanjaro, safari or other experiences.
