How to read the prices Guide prices are estimates in USD per international adult for the group and sharing arrangement shown. Your dates, accommodation, transfers and fees determine the final total. Children, residents and other group sizes receive their own quote. David’s written quote lists the full price, inclusions, extras and booking terms before you confirm.
Can I combine two activities in one day?

Yes. Stone Town with a spice visit, or Jozani with Kuza Cave, can be practical starting ideas. David checks the transfer route, opening times and your pace before quoting; separate card prices do not include a combined transfer automatically.

What happens when the sea is unsuitable?

The captain or activity provider decides whether it is safe to depart. Before payment, your written quote must state the rescheduling and refund terms for weather-related changes. Keep some flexibility around ocean days.

Is the clear kayak included at USD 45?

No. USD 45 is the standard mangrove kayak estimate. A transparent kayak starts at an estimated USD 60 per adult, with venue, equipment and availability confirmed separately.

Are dolphins, reef visibility or an exact hotel guaranteed?

Wildlife and visibility vary. The six-day package uses named guesthouses confirmed for your dates; no hotel is held automatically by viewing the website. The written itinerary confirms the actual services.
